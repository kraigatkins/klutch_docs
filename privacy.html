KLUTCH DIGITAL MARKETING

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Replaces all prior versions

Klutch Digital Marketing ("Klutch," "we," "us") provides marketing, reputation, communication, and analytics services to businesses — primarily automotive dealerships ("Clients"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you. It covers everything we operate: our websites, the Klutch platform, the Klutch Pixel, email and text-message campaigns, call tracking, review management, and our integrations with Google and Meta.

1. Information We Collect

Website visitor data (the Klutch Pixel)

Our Clients may place the Klutch Pixel — a small piece of code — on their websites to understand how visitors use their site. When you visit a site that uses the Klutch Pixel, we collect: pages viewed and page titles, the website that referred you, campaign tags (UTM parameters), the date and time of your visits, your device and browser type, screen size, and a randomly generated visitor identifier stored in your browser. We store only a shortened, hashed version of your IP address — never the full address. If you identify yourself on the site — for example by submitting a form containing your email address, or by arriving from an email we sent on the Client's behalf — your email address may be associated with your visit history for that Client.

Information provided by our Clients

Clients provide us customer records from their own systems (such as dealer management and customer relationship systems, or uploaded files) so we can perform services for them. These records may include name, email address, phone number, postal address, and vehicle or transaction information. We process this information on the Client's behalf as their service provider.

Campaign engagement

When we send email or text messages on a Client's behalf, we record delivery outcomes and engagement: whether a message was delivered, opened, or clicked; bounces; unsubscribe and opt-out requests; and replies such as STOP messages. For tracked phone numbers, we record call metadata: the caller's number, call time, duration, and outcome (answered or missed). We do not record call audio.

Review and social data

With Client authorization, we access business reviews and related public content through the Google Business Profile API and Meta (Facebook) Platform, including reviewer names, ratings, review text, and recommendation status, in order to monitor and respond to reviews on the Client's behalf.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide services to our Clients: sending marketing communications, measuring campaign results, managing reviews, tracking calls, and reporting website activity.
  • To connect related records — for example, recognizing that a website visitor is an existing customer of the Client, or that a customer who received an email later made a purchase — so Clients can measure and improve their marketing.
  • To maintain suppression lists so that people who opt out stay opted out across our platform.
  • To secure, maintain, and improve our systems.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with: (a) the Client on whose behalf it was collected; (b) service providers that help us operate — including Google Cloud (hosting and data processing), Mailgun (email delivery), Twilio (text and voice), Google, and Meta — each bound to use the data only to provide their service; and (c) authorities when required by law. If we engage data partners to enhance Client marketing in the future, any such data will be obtained and used in accordance with applicable law and this policy.

4. Google and Meta Platform Disclosures

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data accessed through Google Business Profile is used solely to provide review-management services to the authorizing Client.

Our use of data received from Meta's Platform complies with the Meta Platform Terms. We access Facebook Page data (including reviews and recommendations) only for Pages that a Client has connected, and only to provide reputation-management services to that Client. You may request deletion of data we obtained through Meta by contacting us (Section 8); we also honor Meta's data-deletion callback requests automatically.

5. Text Messaging (SMS) Terms

Text messages are sent only to individuals whose consent has been obtained in accordance with applicable law. Message frequency varies by program. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for assistance. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with or sold to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Email

Every marketing email we send includes a working unsubscribe link and honors one-click unsubscribe. Opt-outs take effect promptly and are maintained on permanent suppression lists. We comply with the CAN-SPAM Act.

7. Cookies, Pixels, and Your Choices

  • Opt out of pixel measurement: you can block or clear the visitor identifier using your browser's settings (clearing site data / blocking storage for the site), or contact us to have your data removed.
  • Email: use the unsubscribe link in any message.
  • Text: reply STOP.
  • Access or deletion: you may request a copy of, or deletion of, personal information we hold about you (Section 8). Where we process data on a Client's behalf, we may route your request to that Client.

8. Contact & Requests

Email kraig@klutchdigitalmarketing.com for privacy questions, access or deletion requests, or to opt out. We respond to verified requests within the timeframes required by applicable law.

9. Data Retention & Security

We retain information for as long as needed to provide services, honor suppression obligations (opt-out records are kept so opt-outs persist), and meet legal requirements. Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure in the United States with access controls, encryption in transit, and least-privilege practices.

10. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be posted here.
