Program Name

Klutch Review Requests — a customer-feedback text messaging program operated by Klutch Digital Marketing, LLC ("Klutch") on behalf of its participating business clients (each a "Participating Business"), including automotive dealerships such as Lewis Ford (Fayetteville, AR) and McConnell GMC (Mobile, AL).

Program Description

Customers who complete a purchase or service visit at a Participating Business and verbally agree, in person, to receive a follow-up message will receive a single text message thanking them and inviting them to share feedback about their experience through an online review link. Messages are transactional customer-care messages; no promotional or marketing content is sent.

Message Frequency

One (1) message per transaction. This program does not send recurring messages. A customer will only receive an additional message if they complete a new purchase or service transaction and consent again.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. Klutch and the Participating Business do not charge for these messages, but your carrier's standard messaging rates apply.

Opting Out

Reply STOP to any message to opt out immediately. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further messages will be sent. Additional supported opt-out keywords: STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, QUIT, OPTOUT, REVOKE.

Help and Support

Reply HELP or INFO to any message for assistance. You may also contact program support at kraig@klutchdigitalmarketing.com, or contact the Participating Business directly using the contact information on your purchase or service paperwork.

Consent

Consent to receive messages is collected verbally, in person, at the Participating Business at the time of your sales or service transaction, and is recorded (name, mobile number, date, and time) in the business's CRM/DMS system. Consent is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Privacy

Mobile phone numbers and text messaging opt-in data will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our full Privacy Policy for details on how information is collected, used, and protected.

Carrier Disclaimer

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
